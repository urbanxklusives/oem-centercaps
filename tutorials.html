Chinese-market brands — an honest note

We looked hard for genuine center cap tutorials for Chinese-market brands — BYD, Geely, NIO, Chery, Great Wall/Haval — in English and in Chinese. As of today, they don't exist on YouTube: the results are sales listings, reviews and crash tests, not a single real cap tutorial. We won't pad this page with those. The method that works on every other nation's caps works on theirs too — measure first — and when real Chinese-market tutorials appear, they'll be added here.
