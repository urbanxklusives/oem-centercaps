Center Cap Styles — Dome, Flat, Smoothy, Spinner, Rally, Scallop

Style guides · Cap faces from 2.25 in to 12 in

By oemcentercaps.com (JTT Imports) — measured fitment guides for OEM center caps, hub caps and wheel caps.

Center caps are not just a size — they are a style catalog. The faces buyers actually choose between are dome, flat, smoothy, spinner, rally, and scallop, in diameters spanning roughly 2.25 in to 12 in across the market. Knowing the style names matters when you shop, because two caps can share a size and look nothing alike — and two caps can share a look and fit nothing alike.

The six faces, in plain language

A dome rises from the wheel; a flat sits flush. A smoothy is the clean, unbroken face. A spinner carries the moving center that keeps turning after the wheel stops. A rally face nods to motorsport wheels, and a scallop carries the shaped edge. None of these names is a size — they describe what you see, not what fits.

The diameter range tells the rest of the story: car caps cluster in the small tens of millimetres, while truck lug-cover styles run far larger. Style shopping without a size is how a beautiful cap ends up back in its box.

Style is not fitment

Here is the rule that saves the most returns: pick the style you like, then fit it like any other cap. Caliper the face OD, measure the clip ID on the back, and read the molded part number if there is one. A spinner in the wrong size is still the wrong cap — it just looks better while it does not fit.

Materials matter here too: most OEM caps are ABS plastic, with aluminum alloy, stainless, and chrome-plated steel in the mix (see Center Cap Materials). A style you love in a material that will not survive your winters is a short-term win — see Winter Salt-Belt Center Cap Care.
