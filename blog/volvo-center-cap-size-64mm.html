Volvo Center Cap Size — the 64 mm OE Cap

European fitment · Volvo

By oemcentercaps.com (JTT Imports) — measured fitment guides for OEM center caps, hub caps and wheel caps.

Volvo's OE center cap is documented at 64 mm in DEZENT's OE-cap fitment guide — a single, tidy published figure for the brand's mainstream wheels. As always on this site, that number is the starting point for your search, not a substitute for measuring the cap in your hand.

A winter brand's caps

Volvos are bought for winters, and their caps serve through them: salt, slush and freeze-thaw cycles season after season. Factory caps are mostly ABS plastic, which shrugs off the cold well, but clip tips age and embrittle. If one cap has let go, inspect the other three while the wheel is off — clips age as a set. Our salt-belt care guide covers the seasonal routine.

What to check before ordering

  • Measure across the face (OD) and across the clip tips (ID) — the ID is the fitment.
  • Read the part number molded into the back of the old cap and match it where you can.
  • Match the finish to your set; Volvo caps run several face treatments across the years.
Verify before you order: the Cap Finder carries the Volvo entry at 64 mm; confirm on your own wheel with the How to Measure method.
