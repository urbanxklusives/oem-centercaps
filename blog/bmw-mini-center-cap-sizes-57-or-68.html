BMW & Mini Center Cap Sizes — One Brand, Two Caps: 57 mm or 68 mm

European fitment · BMW, Mini

By oemcentercaps.com (JTT Imports) — measured fitment guides for OEM center caps, hub caps and wheel caps.

Ask a parts counter for "a BMW center cap" and you've only finished half the question. BMW — and Mini, which shares the family — run two OE cap sizes: 57 mm and 68 mm, and which one you need is decided by the wheel, not the badge on the hood. Order by model name alone and you have a coin flip's chance of getting a cap that seats.

The 68 mm cap

The larger of the two families measures 68 mm OD and 68 mm ID — face diameter and clip diameter are the same number on this cap, which makes it one of the easier ones to verify with a caliper: measure across the face, then across the clip tips on the back, and both readings should land at 68 mm. If your old cap reads 68 on both sides, you're in this family and can order with confidence.

The 57 mm cap

The smaller family is a 57 mm OE cap, fitted to a different set of wheels. Eleven millimetres is not a tolerance — a 57 mm cap dropped into a 68 mm wheel opening falls straight through the fitment, and a 68 mm cap will never seat in a 57 mm opening no matter how hard you push. There is no "close enough" anywhere in this brand.

Why the split exists

Center caps are designed for wheels, and wheel designs change across generations, diameters and trim packages. BMW has shipped a lot of wheels over the years, and the cap families followed the wheels. This is the same pattern you'll see across this whole site: the vehicle is the starting point, the wheel is the answer. Two identical-looking cars on different factory wheels can take different caps.

Don't let the hub bore vote

A BMW 3 Series wheel centers on a 72.6 mm hub bore (5×120). That number is real — and it matches neither cap family. The bore centers the wheel on the hub; the cap clips into its own opening. Ordering a 72.6 mm "cap" because a fitment chart printed the bore is the single most common wrong-order story in this business, and BMW's numbers make the trap especially easy to fall into because 72.6 sits above both real cap sizes and looks plausible.

The 30-second sort: pop one cap, caliper the face and the clip tips. 68/68 — large family. 57 across the face — small family. No cap to measure? Run your vehicle through the Cap Finder to see the sourced fitment, then confirm against the wheel opening using the method on the How to Measure page.

Before you buy

Read the back of your old cap first — BMW moldings usually carry a part number, and a number beats a guess every time. Then match OD and clip ID, in that order. If you're shopping marketplaces, search by the size family plus the part number, not by "BMW center cap" alone; the listings that publish full dimensions are the ones worth your money.
