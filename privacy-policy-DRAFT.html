Privacy Policy — OEM Center Caps

Effective date: October 27, 2026  |  Site: oemcentercaps.com

OEM Center Caps is a Canadian-run, free fitment resource. We do not sell center caps and we do not stock them. We run on a supplier and affiliate model: we publish fitment sizes and guides for free, and some of our "where to buy" links earn us a commission if you buy through them. This policy explains, in plain language, what this site collects, what it does not collect, and how to reach us.

1. What we collect — and what we do not

What you send us on purpose:

  • Cap Concierge requests. If you use the Cap Concierge form, you send us your name, your email address, your vehicle year/make/model, an OEM part number if you have one, your measured cap size if you have taken it, how many caps you need, an optional photo of your wheel or cap, and anything else you write in the message box. We use that information only to answer your request and find your cap. We do not sell it, rent it, or share it with advertisers.
  • Email. If you email us at jtt.imports@hotmail.com — for a fitment question, a correction, or a review — we receive whatever you put in that email, and we use it to reply to you.

What we do not collect:

  • We do not run Google Analytics on this site, so we are not collecting analytics profiles about your visits through Google Analytics.
  • We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not take payments on this site, so we do not collect passwords, payment card details, or billing addresses.
  • The free Cap Finder tool runs in your browser. It does not ask for your name or email, and it does not submit a form to us. The measurements you type into it stay on your device while you use it.

2. Cookies

This site itself sets no login or tracking cookies of its own. However, third-party services on this site may set cookies in your browser — see the next section. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time; the guides and the Cap Finder will still work, though some third-party features (such as ads) may behave differently.

3. Advertising and affiliate links

Google AdSense. This site carries Google AdSense advertising code. Google may use cookies to show you ads and to measure them. At the time of writing, our AdSense account is still in Google's review process and has not been approved, so no ad revenue is being earned yet — but the code is present on the site, so Google's ad practices may already apply. You can read Google's advertising privacy information at policies.google.com/technologies/ads and opt out of personalized advertising through Google's Ads Settings.

Amazon Associates links (affiliate disclosure). Some links on our Where to Buy page and our fitment guide go to Amazon and carry our Associates tag (uxk-20). If you buy through one of those links, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases. Amazon sets its own cookies when you click through, and Amazon's privacy notice governs what Amazon does with that visit.

eBay links. Some links on this site are plain searches on eBay. They are not affiliate links today, so we earn nothing from them. If that changes, this policy will be updated.

Other third-party links. Our guides link out to manufacturers, retailers, and reference pages. Once you leave oemcentercaps.com, those sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for them.

4. Hosting and technical logs

Like most websites, our hosting service keeps basic technical logs — such as which pages were requested and when — to keep the site running and to diagnose problems. We do not use those logs to identify individual visitors or to build profiles about you.

5. Children's privacy

This site is a vehicle-parts reference for adults buying and measuring center caps. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us at jtt.imports@hotmail.com and we will delete it.

6. Changes to this policy

If how we collect or use information changes — for example, if analytics is added, if a newsletter is added, or if affiliate programs change — we will update this page and change the effective date at the top. The version on this page is the current one.

7. Contact

Questions, corrections, or a request to delete a Cap Concierge submission you sent us:

Email: jtt.imports@hotmail.com
Instagram: @oemcentercaps
