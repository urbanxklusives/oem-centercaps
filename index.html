The right center cap is a measurement,
not a vehicle name.

A center cap is the small disc that snaps into the middle of your wheel, covering the hub and axle nut. It looks simple — until you order one by year and model, it arrives, and it's 2 mm too small. Center caps are sized to the wheel, not the truck or car, and the same model can wear a dozen different wheels.

Our promise: measure before you buy. Every chart on this site is a starting point, not a guarantee. We show you the sizes that are commonly correct, explain why they vary, and teach you the 60-second caliper check that settles it for good.

What is an OEM center cap?

An OEM (Original Equipment Manufacturer) center cap is the factory cap designed for a specific wheel. It does three jobs:

  • Protection — it seals the center bore against water, road salt, brake dust and grit that corrode the hub and axle nut.
  • Retention safety — a correctly sized cap locks in with an interference fit. A wrong-size cap rattles, sits crooked, or pops off at highway speed.
  • Finish — it's the badge in the middle of every wheel. A missing cap makes a clean truck look neglected.

Most factory caps are ABS plastic with plastic snap-in clips or a metal spring ring. Trucks and older wheels also use push-through caps and big lug-cover caps that span the whole lug pattern. Each style is covered in the Fitment Guide.

Why fit is everything

1 mm is the difference

Center cap sizing is metric and tight. A cap that's 1 mm small won't lock; 1 mm big won't seat. There is no "close enough."

Same truck, different cap

An F-150 can carry a 3-inch snap-in cap on one factory wheel and a 180 mm lug-cover cap on another. Trim and wheel option decide the cap — not the badge on the fender.

Two numbers get mixed up

Sellers confuse cap diameter with the wheel's hub center bore (87.1 mm on an F-150). They're different measurements. Mixing them up is the #1 cause of wrong orders — we explain both in the guide.

A Canadian brand, running caps worldwide

oemcentercaps.com is run from Canada — and built for everywhere. The big US catalogs ship free to 50 states and stop at the border. We don't stop anywhere: our brand coverage spans North American trucks, European cars, Japanese and Korean fleets, and the grey-import and expat wheels that don't show up in domestic catalogs at all.

Forget the tariffs — OEM center caps are small, light, and shipped worldwide by suppliers and marketplaces on every continent. We stock nothing and sell nothing directly: we publish the fitment knowledge, then connect you to the suppliers and affiliates who have your cap, wherever you and your wheels happen to be. How that model works — honestly, including how we earn — is on the Where to Buy page.

Canadian winter note: road salt eats cheap chrome plating and brittle no-name plastic. If you run winter wheels in the salt belt, favour quality ABS or coated metal caps — and pop your caps off before the tire shop's impact tools do it for you.
