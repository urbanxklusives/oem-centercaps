Cap Concierge

We find your cap.

Some caps don't come to the caliper: discontinued, dealer-obsolete, or buried in marketplace listings under the wrong name. Tell us the cap you need — vehicle, year, OEM part number if you know it, and photos — and we hunt it down at the lowest price we can source. You approve the total before a dollar moves.

How it works

  1. Send the request. Use the form below (or email) with your vehicle year/make/model, the OEM part number if you have it, your measured size if you've taken it, and photos — the wheel face, and the back of a surviving cap if one exists.
  2. We source it. We search our supplier and marketplace channels for a genuine OEM cap matching your part number and measurements — not a look-alike. If the exact cap can't be sourced, we say so and tell you the closest honest options.
  3. You approve the price. We quote one total: the sourced price plus our finder's fee. The fee is quoted per cap, up front, as part of that total — no fixed schedule, no surprises, and no payment until you approve the quote.
  4. We get it to you. On your approval, we complete the purchase and arrange delivery, and keep you posted until the cap is in your hands.

What to send us

The essentials

Vehicle year, make and model; which wheel position if it matters; how many caps you need.

The identifiers

The OEM part number molded on the back of the cap (a photo of the back is perfect), plus your measurements if you've taken them — OD, clip ID, clip depth, in millimetres.

The photos

The wheel face with the cap missing or in place, and the back of any surviving cap. Photos settle what descriptions argue about.

Honest terms: we source genuine OEM caps. The price we quote is the total you'll pay — cap, shipping to you, and our finder's fee combined — confirmed with you before any payment. If we can't source your cap, you pay nothing and we tell you where we looked.
